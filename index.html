Have another question?
Talk to the pilot team

Is ClearPath part of the VA? +

No. Veteran ClearPath is an independent product from Fathom Shield Solutions. It is not a VA service, and it does not make VA benefit decisions.

Do I need an existing claim or decision letter? +

No. You can start with your own experiences and supporting documents. If you have a VA decision letter, ClearPath analyzes it for you, identifies issues and outcomes, and points you back to the source so you can review the findings.

Will ClearPath file my claim or guarantee a rating? +

No. ClearPath helps you understand information and prepare for a conversation with your VSO. It does not submit claims to the VA or promise a particular rating or outcome.

Who can join the pilot? +

The current pilot is focused on veterans and participating VSOs in Arizona. Contact the pilot team about participation. Staff accounts require an invitation and verification.

Should I email my records to the team? +

No. Use email for general questions only. Keep medical records, claim documents, Social Security numbers, and account codes out of email. Participants upload documents through the ClearPath application.
